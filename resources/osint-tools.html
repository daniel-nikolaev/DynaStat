What is OSINT?

Open-source intelligence (OSINT) is information gathered from publicly available sources, such as websites, public records, maps and social media. It's used in research, journalism, cybersecurity and investigations.

What the directory will include

Every tool listed will be free to use. Tools will be grouped by category so you can find the right one quickly. Planned categories include:

  • Search engines and advanced search techniques
  • Username and public profile lookup
  • Domain, website and IP address research
  • Image analysis and geolocation
  • Web archives and historical snapshots

Responsible use

These tools should only be used for legal and ethical purposes. Respect people's privacy and follow the terms of service of every tool you use.
