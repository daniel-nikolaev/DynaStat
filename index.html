Health & Safety

Medication Allergy LookupWorking title · Online tool

Search for a specific medication and see which allergies could cause a reaction to it. For example, common progesterone capsules are made with peanut oil, so people with peanut allergies may not be able to take them. Information like this is hard to find, and this tool puts it in one place.

  • Search any medication by name
  • See which allergies may cause a reaction
  • Surfaces ingredient information that isn't readily available
