How it works

  1. Pick your products Select or search for the items in your store that need restocking.
  2. ezRestock matches suppliers Each item is matched to the supplier you order it from.
  3. Place your orders See exactly who to order from, with no guesswork.

Features

  • Select or search for the products you need to restock
  • Matches every item to the right supplier
  • Saves time and cuts the guesswork out of ordering

Who it's for

Store owners and managers who restock from several suppliers and want a faster way to know who to order from.
