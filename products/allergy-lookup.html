For information only. This tool won't replace medical advice. Always check with a doctor or pharmacist before taking a new medication.

Why it matters

Some medications contain ingredients that can cause a reaction in people with certain allergies, and this information isn't always easy to find. For example, common progesterone capsules are made with peanut oil, so people with peanut allergies may not be able to take them.

How it works

  1. Search a medication Type in the name of the medication you're looking for.
  2. See possible reactions View which allergies may cause a reaction to it.
  3. Talk to your provider Bring what you find to your doctor or pharmacist.

Features

  • Search any medication by name
  • See which allergies may cause a reaction
  • Surfaces ingredient information that isn't readily available
